Zero-Server Privacy Guarantee

Zero Files Ever Leave Your Browser

PixelShield is engineered specifically for sensitive identity documents, passports, and private photos.

1. HTML5 In-Memory Canvas Processing: Images are decoded into standard HTML5 ImageBitmaps held strictly in transient browser RAM.
2. Automatic EXIF & Metadata Purging: Redrawing pixels onto a pristine <canvas> context completely discards GPS, camera serials, and timestamps.
3. Zero Cloud Ingestion / Zero Telemetry: No tracking scripts, no third-party analytics, and zero API calls transmitting binary payloads.
